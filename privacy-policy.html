Pul & Zar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Pul & Zar does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app never connects to the internet and requires no account, registration or sign-in.
  • Your settings, game statistics and the saved match are stored only on this computer, in the app's own folder. This data never leaves your device.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking technology and no third-party software libraries are used.
  • The e-mail link in the "About" section only opens your Mail app; the app never sees your e-mail address.
